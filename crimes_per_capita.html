How to Use This Analysis

  • Use the dropdown menu to filter by specific years or view all-time data
  • Hover over bars to see exact crime counts, rates, and population estimates
  • The default view shows all-time rates from 2009 to present
  • Neighborhoods are sorted by crime rate (highest to lowest)
  • Click on neighborhood boundaries in the map to see detailed crime statistics
  • Map colors correspond to the bar chart colors for easy reference
  • Population estimates are based on area, density, and housing patterns
  • Data includes only violent crimes: homicide, assault, robbery, kidnapping, arson, weapons violations, threats, stalking, and extortion

Data Source: Cambridge Open Data Portal
