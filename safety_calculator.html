How the Safety Score Works

  • Base Score (0-10): Calculated from recent violent crime rates in each neighborhood
  • Time Factors: Daytime hours are safer, late night hours increase risk
  • Day Factors: Weekdays are generally safer than weekends
  • Transportation: Driving is safest, walking has highest exposure
  • Final Score: Combines all factors for a personalized safety assessment
  • Data Source: Based on violent crimes (2020-present) from Cambridge Open Data

Data Source: Cambridge Open Data Portal

Safety scores are estimates based on historical crime data and should not be the sole factor in safety decisions
